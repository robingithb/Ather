Aether Demo Sessions

Mountain Drive (Demo), Coastal Road (Demo), Evening Lights (Demo), Ocean Air
(Demo), Midnight City (Demo), and Golden Hour (Demo) are original synthesized
instrumentals generated for this application. They use no third-party samples,
recordings, or existing songs. Each PCM WAV is 24 seconds. The three original
asset paths and audio files remain unchanged for previously saved queues.

The reproducible generator is tools/generate_demo_audio.py in the source tree.
The application labels these tracks as bundled demos. They play only after the
user chooses a demo or the demo collection. Choosing a recommendation preserves
selected documents and adds only missing bundled originals. The catalogue does
not represent an attached device or external music service. Cover images are
bundled Aether design artwork assigned to these original demo instrumentals.
